  • Created a sensitive groups MFA policy.

4. Identity Governance Access Review Lab

Built a Microsoft Entra Identity Governance access review lab with live access review creation, contractor group review, reviewer decisions, remediation tracking, least privilege analysis, and audit evidence documentation.

Identity Governance Access Reviews Least Privilege Remediation Tracking Audit Evidence

Problem

Organizations need to regularly verify that users still need access to sensitive groups, applications, and privileged roles. Without regular reviews, users may keep access longer than necessary.

What I Built

  • Reviewed the SG-Contractors group.
  • Assigned a reviewer to evaluate contractor access.
  • Approved contractor access that was still needed.
  • Denied contractor access that was no longer required.
  • Created remediation tracking documentation.
  • Created a risk matrix and audit evidence packet.
  • Collected access review screenshots as audit evidence.
